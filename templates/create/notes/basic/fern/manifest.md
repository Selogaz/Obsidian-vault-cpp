A fern has grown beyond a rough capture. The main idea is clear, but the note still needs more explanation, evidence, or examples.

A fern usually has

- a clear central idea
- enough content to continue working
- gaps that still limit understanding

Move it to Incubator when the idea is developed enough to connect with related notes.
