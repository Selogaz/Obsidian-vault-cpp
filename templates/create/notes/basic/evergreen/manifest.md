An evergreen note has found a stable home in the knowledge network. It is clear enough to reuse and connected enough to rediscover through related notes.

An evergreen usually has

- one clear idea written in your own words
- a stable place among related notes
- links that help you find and develop related ideas

An evergreen note can still change as you find better wording, evidence, or connections.
