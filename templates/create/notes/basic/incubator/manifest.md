An incubator note is being developed and prepared for a place in the knowledge network. Work here focuses on making the idea stronger, linking it to related notes, and deciding where it belongs.

An incubator usually has

- a clear idea with enough content to build on
- several related notes worth connecting
- no stable home in the network yet

Move it to Evergreen when its home and useful connections are clear.
